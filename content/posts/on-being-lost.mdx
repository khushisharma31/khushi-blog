There is a particular kind of lost that doesn't feel like confusion.

It feels like standing very still while everything around you keeps moving. You know who you are. You know what you love. You just don't know where to put it anymore.

Plans fell through. Not because I wasn't ready. Not because I wasn't good enough. Just — circumstances. The kind that have no villain, no lesson, no clean ending. Just a door that closed.

And I've been sitting with that.

What I've found is this: the lost feeling isn't the enemy. It's a clearing. Nothing is growing there yet, but the ground is soft and something in me is still reaching — upward, sideways, toward anything warm.

I don't have an answer. I'm not going to write one here.

But I do have this: a faint spark that refuses to go out. And I've decided that's enough to follow.
