Some mornings the code compiles
and the words flow
and I feel like two rivers meeting —

neither one asked
to share a bed,
but here,
where they touch,
the water is warmer.

I have been told
these two things don't go together.
The circuit and the stanza.
The model and the metaphor.

I have found
they were always
the same question —

*how do you hold something*
*that keeps changing shape*
*and call it yours?*
